# A personal data breach

A breach is personal data reaching someone it should not, being lost, or being changed without permission: a message sent
to the wrong families, a leaked key, a lost laptop with an export.

## The first hour

1. **Stop it.** Pause what is leaking: a connection (Integrations → the connection → Pause), a school (the school →
   Actions → Pause), a module's kill switch (Plans and modules). Rotate any leaked key ([rotate-a-secret.md](rotate-a-secret.md)).
2. **Record it.** Console → Compliance → New → Breach, naming each school affected. The record starts the 72-hour clock
   for telling each school (and, through them, the regulator) and keeps who did what.
3. **Find out the scope:** which schools, which people, what data, since when. Integrations → Activity and the schools'
   audit logs show who received what.

## Telling the school

The school, as controller, tells its regulator and the people affected; Tangent tells the school without delay and within
the clock. Compliance → the breach → **Notify**: the school's contact receives the notice (the wording comes from the
country pack: what happened, what data, what Tangent did, whom to contact), and the record shows when.

Regulator deadlines differ by country: 72 hours from awareness under UK GDPR; India's DPDP rules and the UAE's PDPL ask for
notice without delay. The school's pack names them; Tangent's own deadline to the school is the 72-hour clock.

## After

Close the record with what was done and what changes so it cannot happen again (Compliance → the breach → Close). Write
the change into the code or this runbook in the same week.
