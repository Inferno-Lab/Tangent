# A request for a copy, a correction or deletion of personal data

The school is the controller of its families' and staff's data; Tangent processes it for the school. Requests go to the
school, which answers them; Tangent helps when asked.

## Where requests arrive

- Families ask in the app (Settings → Privacy → Ask for a copy / a correction / to delete it, or Withdraw my consent).
- The school's office records requests made in person or by letter: admin → Privacy → Log a request.
- Console → Compliance → Data requests lists every school's open requests and how long they have waited.

## Answering (the school's office)

1. **Copy (access).** The office gathers the person's records from where they live: the student's record (Students →
   the student), the fee statement and receipts (Fees), the report cards (Exams), attendance (Reports → Attendance,
   filtered to the class). It sends them through its usual channel, then answers the request (Privacy → the request →
   Answer → Done, saying what was sent). There is no one-click export of one person's data yet.
2. **Correction.** The office corrects the record where it lives and answers the request with what changed.
3. **Deletion.** What the law makes the school keep (fee receipts, attendance registers for the country's minimum) is
   kept. A student who has left is erased with the other leavers past the school's retention period: console → the
   school → Actions → Erase leavers past retention (a second person approves, and the school's slug is typed to confirm).
   The office answers the request saying what was erased and what is kept, and why.
4. **Withdrawn consent.** The app records it and opens a deletion request; the office answers it as above.

A request the school will not carry out is answered with Decline and the reason (the family reads it).

## Deadlines

The country's legal deadline applies (for example one month under UK GDPR). Answer within it, and note any extension and
its reason in the answer.
